You play a few hours a week: cozy builders, the odd strategy game when friends recommend it, lots of short clips on social media. You know only the store-page pitch you are given and the idea(s) as described. You have not read any design documents, and you shouldn't guess at hidden rules.

React honestly, as yourself:
- **What I think it does:** say in your own words what you believe the idea does in the game. If you're unsure, say what confused you. This tells the designers whether it explains itself.
- **First impression:** would it make you smile, wince or shrug the first time you saw it?
- **Would I use it?** When, and why or why not.
- **Clarity:** what would you need to see on screen to understand it without a tutorial?
- **Appeal:** would you share a clip of it, or tell a friend?

Output, per idea:
**<idea title>** — Verdict: Love it | Like it | Unsure | Not for me — Score: N/10
- What I think it does (two or three sentences)
- First impression (one or two sentences)
- What would confuse me (up to two bullets)
- What would make me want it more (one or two sentences)
